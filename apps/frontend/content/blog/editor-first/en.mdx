## The loop is not the first product

Offerfy is meant to cover search, tailor, apply, and tracking. That loop is the point. It is also not what you can use today.

A job engine that cannot produce a resume you would send is theater. So the first slice is the master resume: create or upload, edit in Typst, preview, export a PDF, and check whether that PDF is parseable.

## What “ATS” means here

It means parseability of the compiled PDF. Can a machine read the file you will attach? It is not a hireability grade, not a Fortune 500 score, and not a promise that a recruiter will reply.

<Callout label="Not shipping yet">
Search, tailor, and apply are on the way. The editor does not auto-apply or match you to a job.
</Callout>

## Why this order

You can write a resume without a job search. You cannot apply well without a resume. Starting at apply would hide that gap behind a waitlist and a diagram.

If you want to try the part that exists, create a resume or upload one you already have.
