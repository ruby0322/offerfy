## The AI that already knows you can now get in

You probably already have an assistant that knows what you work on, where you want to go, and which parts of your history belong on a resume. Until now it has been outside Offerfy, and you have been the one carrying things between them.

Not any more. Offerfy runs an MCP server. You mint a key, paste it at your own assistant, and it is in.

## Connecting: paste one block

There is a copyable prompt at the top of [the connection page](/en/mcp). Replace the placeholder with your key and paste the whole thing at your assistant. If it is Claude Code or any coding agent, it will set up the connection itself and then tell you which tools it can see.

claude.ai takes a few clicks instead, because connectors are added in a web form that the assistant cannot reach on its own. Those steps are on the same page.

## What it can do

Fifteen tools in five groups: search jobs and read the matches already scored for you, read and edit resumes (including forking a tailored copy for one posting), publish a resume as a public link, mint a PDF download link, and read or replace your job preferences.

## You can see what it did

This is the part we cared most about. A key acts as you, but only on your own account, and every call is written down: which tool, when, and whether it succeeded or was refused. Expand any key in Settings to read its log. Don't like what you see? Revoke it, and it stops immediately.

We keep what was done, not what your assistant said.

## Try it

Mint a key in Settings, then open [the connection page](/en/mcp).
