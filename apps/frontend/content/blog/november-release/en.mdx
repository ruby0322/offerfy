## Your whole search on one page

Overview, applications, matches, resumes, profile, usage — six tabs. The first line tells you what has arrived since you last looked, and then it gives you the one thing to do today.

<Figure src="01-dashboard.png" alt="The Offerfy dashboard overview: six tabs, the next action, this week's AI allowance, and matching roles" />

## After you apply, the numbers do the talking

Every application is logged, including the ones nobody answered. Reply rate, median days to reply, which ones have been quiet too long — not a feeling, your own data.

The flow diagram splits each batch into interviews, replies and silence, so "I applied to a lot" becomes a shape you can actually look at.

<Figure src="02-applications.png" alt="The applications page: 40% reply rate, 5 waiting, a median of 6 days to reply, and the application flow diagram" />

## Tailoring doesn't write it for you — it tells you what's missing

The agent maps every requirement in the posting against your resume and says plainly which ones are there and which are not. Where there is something to rewrite, one button applies it.

<Figure src="04-editor.png" alt="The editor: four suggestions, two appliable, two that are questions; on the right, the live-compiled resume passing 8 of 8 ATS checks" />

Where there isn't, it asks you instead. It will not invent a fact your resume doesn't contain — it's your resume, and every line in it has to be true.

<Figure src="04b-asks.png" alt="Two suggestions that ask a question instead of rewriting, each with one-tap answers" />

<Callout label="Why it works this way">
A tool that quietly adds "experienced with GPU cluster scheduling" hands you a resume you can't defend in an interview. Better that it asks.
</Callout>

## A weekly shortlist that stays empty when nothing fits

Only the roles you are most likely to want and actually fit. Each one says why it was picked and where the gap is. A week with no real match is an empty week.

<Figure src="03-matches.png" alt="The matches page: five roles picked this week, each with a score, a reason and a gap" />

On a phone it's one role to a page, by swipe.

<Figure src="07-mobile-matches.png" alt="The matches page on a phone: one role per card" variant="peek" />

## The catalogue now holds 95 employers

All of it from official career pages and five ATS platforms, rescanned four times a day. Non-technical roles never get in. Every employer has a page of its own.

<Figure src="05-employers.png" alt="The employers page: 95 companies hiring, each with its open-role count and what it added this week" />
